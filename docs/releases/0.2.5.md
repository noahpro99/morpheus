# Morpheus 0.2.5

Desktop only: Linux, Windows and macOS (Apple silicon and Intel). The phone apps are paused until they are rebuilt natively; the last ones are in 0.2.2.

## Cameras and video
- Cameras on Windows and macOS: the camera picker, the Settings preview and calls use your webcam (USB, built-in, and Continuity Camera on macOS). Unplugging and plugging back in picks the same camera up again, and hanging up turns the camera light off.
- Hardware video on Windows (the GPU's H.264 encoder: Intel Quick Sync, AMD and NVIDIA) and macOS (VideoToolbox), falling back to software when there is none.
- Settings > Voice & Video > "Hardware video acceleration", on by default; off sends VP8 in software. Diagnostics says which encoder is in use, and the camera in use.

## Windows
- One title bar, the app's own: drag it, snap it to screen edges, double-click to maximise, and hover maximise for Windows 11's snap layouts.
- Closing the window (the X, Alt+F4, the taskbar) hides it to the tray instead of quitting; Quit from the tray quits.
- Start on login (Settings > Startup), optionally hidden in the tray.
- The tray icon follows the taskbar's light or dark theme.
- The taskbar button shows an unread badge and flashes on a new DM or mention while the window isn't in front.
- The microphone no longer drops audio after a short stall, and noise suppression that can't keep up steps down instead of cutting out.
- Diagnostics shows memory, the Windows version, the GPU and the refresh rate.

## Everywhere
- The tray icon shows a red unread badge; the macOS Dock and Linux launchers (that support it) show the count.
- The server's icon sits in the top bar before the server's name; outside a server, the Morpheus logo.
- Hanging up clears the call badge on the server at once, and others see you leave right away.
- Profile and call card colours take the picture's main colour instead of a muddy average.
- The app opens in your theme, without a flash of the default one.
- The streamer mode control shows only while sharing your screen or while streamer mode is on.
