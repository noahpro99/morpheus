# Morpheus 0.2.8

Desktop (Linux, Windows and macOS on Apple silicon and Intel) and, again, Android: the phone app is back, rebuilt natively. Morpheus is in alpha, and says so at the top of the window.

## Android, rebuilt natively
- A new native app over the same Rust core as the desktop: servers, channels and DMs, the timeline with pictures, files and video, replies, edits, reactions, threads, link previews, emoji and custom emoji, mentions, typing and read receipts, voice and video calls, push notifications, encrypted rooms with device verification and key recovery, bridges, and the desktop's settings groups with a search bar.
- Bottom bar: Servers, DMs in the centre, and You with your own picture. Long-press You to switch accounts, double-tap it to go to the next one.
- It opens straight to where you left off, with the open room's messages already drawn, and pictures come from the phone's cache.
- Swipe from any list to the chat you opened last; the chat slides in over the lists, and the keyboard is up and the message box focused when it does.
- Long-press anything (a message, channel, DM, server, folder or member) for a sheet with the same items as the desktop's right-click menu, with a haptic. Double-tap a message to reply to it; a tap does nothing.
- Share to Morpheus from any app: text, links, pictures, video and files, with the search at the bottom of the screen and the best match next to it.
- Squircle server icons and the desktop's icons and look; the rail and lists can be pulled down so the top is in reach of your thumb.
- Sending is reliable, including right after opening the app, and failed sends can be retried.

## Calls and voice
- Keyboard clicks and other noise between words are kept out in every noise suppression mode, without clipping the start or end of words. Settings > Voice & Video has Discord's "Automatically determine input sensitivity", or set it by hand on a live level meter.
- Fewer dropouts: the microphone thread runs at real-time priority, and encrypted calls recover from lost packets (2% loss went from 1.9% of the audio lost to 0.15%).
- On a voice channel's page, Enter joins the call, and the palette can join a voice channel by name.
- Windows: Share screen opens the screen and window picker at once, with the share settings along its bottom, and one click starts sharing.
- A call card shows one mute icon, never two, and no LIVE badge (the channel list keeps its own). A focused share's placeholder has the sharer's colour, and the call view has the same background as a text channel.
- Someone whose app is killed leaves the call for everyone quickly, and camera and LIVE indicators update for everyone.
- macOS: the camera no longer freezes in Mission Control or while dragging the window.

## Status
- Your status follows the Steam game you're playing ("Playing ..."), and goes back to what it was when you stop (also after a crash). Other programs can set it too through a local socket, for example a browser extension for music (docs/activity-ipc.md).

## Messages
- Loading older messages no longer moves what you're looking at, and dragging the scrollbar, Page Down and End all reach the real bottom.
- Select and copy several messages at once, with names and timestamps.
- Markdown formatting when sending and showing.
- Switching rooms is faster than Discord's (about 34 ms against 36 ms).
- Ctrl+End and Ctrl+Home work in the thread panel too.

## Look and feel
- Transparent (formerly Frosted glass): an opacity slider down to 25%, background only, working on Linux, and applied at once.
- Settings > Appearance: the colour picker sits behind a palette button next to the hex box.
- Custom emoji and server pictures use the same crop step, and file pickers offer only the file types that fit.
- Text on coloured buttons and badges is always readable, in every theme and accent.
- The account switcher shows profile pictures, each account in its own theme colour, and the same account can no longer be listed twice.
- Channel drag-to-reorder feels like the server rail's, with one drop spot between channels.
- The voice chat panel can be resized, its rows aren't cut off, and its emoji button stays put.
- Every window closes the same way: Escape, the X or a click outside.

## Command palette
- Context actions from the right-click menus (e.g. Leave room), What's new, and joining voice channels.
- Actions match other words for them: "reboot" finds Restart Morpheus.
- Mute server and Notifications: Mute are named more clearly.

## Fixes
- macOS: signing in no longer asks for the keychain password over and over, and the GPU is named in diagnostics (Windows too).
- A crash when signing in after switching accounts many times (too many open files) is fixed.
- The sign-in form scrolls in short windows, right-clicking a picture always opens its menu, and the bridges QR code appears.
- Help > Report a bug is a real button.
