# Morpheus 0.2.6

Desktop only: Linux, Windows and macOS (Apple silicon and Intel). The phone apps are paused until they are rebuilt natively; the last ones are in 0.2.2.

## Calls and video
- A floating call window (picture-in-picture) keeps your camera or screen share in view while you're elsewhere in the app.
- Voice channel grid: cards fill the height available. With a focused card, it sits at the top and the others take the rest of the height.
- Hardware video on Windows and Linux, with one place for it: Settings > Voice & Video > Graphics card (hardware acceleration, and on Linux the dedicated GPU switch).
- Screen share audio can leave out Morpheus and Discord (a setting).
- Noise suppression offers a low-delay model (10 ms instead of 30). It downloads the first time you pick it; the standard model stays built in.
- The Linux camera picture is no longer all green.
- Voice channel page: the chat button has no background, and the open chat has its own X.
- The mini call window lost its yellow border, and its invite button is an icon.
- Windows screen picker: a hint says double-clicking a screen or window starts sharing.

## Streamer mode
- It also turns on while another app is capturing your screen, such as a Discord stream or OBS (Linux for now).
- A clearer "Open <channel>?" prompt: the channel or server shown with its icon, a switch to turn streamer mode off, and Cancel (Esc), "Don't ask again during this stream" and Open (Enter).
- Channels you've already opened during a stream open without asking again (a setting, on by default).

## Getting around
- Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab go to the next and previous server.
- Alt+Shift+Down goes to unread rooms in other servers too, and gives a small shake when nothing is unread.
- The quick switcher (Ctrl+K) finds servers by name, and is only as tall as its results.
- matrix.to and matrix: links open in Morpheus; it registers itself for matrix: links on Linux and Windows.
- The channel list can be made wider or narrower by dragging its edge.
- A draggable scrollbar in channels, DMs, threads and the voice chat. It keeps following the mouse outside the panel while you drag.
- "Jump to present" shows its Esc shortcut, and Esc jumps in threads and the voice chat too.
- Server invites show in your DM with the person, and Right-click > Invite to server shows each server's icon.

## Look and feel
- Text and icons on coloured buttons stay readable in every theme and custom colour.
- Theme history: go back to a theme you used before.
- Text channels use a real # icon, the size of the voice speaker; the Rooms button on the rail uses it too.
- The server overview shows the server's icon.
- Member list rows look like the DM list, with headings lined up with the pictures. Pictures no longer flash a letter on blue before loading.
- Message times sit on the same line as the sender's name. Date dividers read "Nov 8".
- The message bar sits lower, lined up with the member list and the user panel.
- Settings search results stay inside their column and scroll when there are many.
- The photo viewer hides its arrows while zoomed in.
- The Secure Backup offer no longer covers other buttons.
- Windows and Linux: the window buttons take clicks over the full height of the title bar, and Close reaches the corner.
- The sign-in page scrolls in a short window, and the keyboard shortcuts window is no longer cut off.

## Faster and lighter
- Big rooms no longer stall the app, members load lazily, and full-size avatars aren't downloaded.
- Lower memory use: decoded pictures are kept within a budget, oversized pictures are shrunk, fewer rooms stay loaded, and fewer worker threads run (in our test, 30 picture-heavy rooms went from about 700 MB to under 400 MB).
- A setting to keep running in the background when the window is closed (on by default).
