# Morpheus 0.2.4

Desktop only: Linux, Windows and macOS (Apple silicon and Intel). The phone apps are paused until they are rebuilt natively; the last ones are in 0.2.2.

**Encrypted calls changed.** To work with Element Call and Element X, encrypted calls now derive and exchange their media keys the way Element does. Encrypted calls between 0.2.4 and 0.2.3 or older won't decrypt, so update everyone (the app updates itself). Unencrypted calls are unaffected.

## Calls
- Element Call and Element X users can join Morpheus calls and the other way round: names, keys sent to each device, key rotation, voice or video call intent, and the stable call-service lookup all match Element now.
- Calls work across homeservers: Morpheus listens on every participant's call server, as Element does.
- Joining retries a busy call service and shows a clear "Could not join" panel with Try again, instead of failing silently.
- Quitting the app hangs up first, so you don't stay in the call as a ghost.
- Hardware video: NVIDIA (NVENC/NVDEC) and Intel/AMD (VA-API) encoders and decoders on Linux, with a fallback to software.
- Fixed a GPU crash ("GPU has crashed") in calls with several screen shares and cameras. Laptops with two GPUs now draw with the dedicated one (Settings > Appearance > Graphics).
- macOS shares your screen's sound (macOS 13 and later). Windows gets a picker for which screen or window to share.
- Right-click a person's card in a call for their menu, their volume and "mute for me"; right-click a screen share for its own volume and mute. Per-person volumes are remembered.
- The mic and headphones right-click menus hold only input or output, with sliders you can grab and double-click to reset, and noise suppression. Advanced noise suppression settings in Voice & Video.
- Double-click someone in a voice channel to focus them. Call buttons are squircles that press down. Call cards take the colour of the person's picture. The speaking ring is on the card only.
- Windows: calls no longer turn other apps' sound down. Strong noise suppression no longer chops the quiet end of speech; microphones with large buffers aren't cut; the macOS mic opens at the device's own rate and fades in (no click or buzz).
- The voice channel chat fills its panel in big windows.

## Profiles and settings
- My Account: change your name and picture (with cropping), Manage account on your server's website. Your picture and name sit at the top of Settings.
- Name and picture changes show everywhere without a restart; a person whose picture is missing in one place uses their other one.
- Profile cards don't jump or move while they load, use the picture's colour, and show "Admin" and mutual servers without a misleading 0.
- Custom theme colour: hex box, swatches and hue/saturation/value sliders, with the whole palette derived from it and text kept readable.
- Diagnostics in Help & Feedback (copy all, included in bug reports), and F3 for a stats overlay.
- Recovery key: confirm before replacing it, and reset it when it's lost.
- Tab and Shift+Tab move through every form.

## Servers, chats and streamer mode
- A welcome screen when you first open a server (also Server overview in its menu) with Join buttons on each channel.
- The server's icon in the top bar. Server invites only in DMs and the inbox.
- Message times say Today, Yesterday and "x min ago"; the time beside grouped messages is just the clock.
- "Say hi! 👋" in an empty DM; copy a link to your profile from New Message; removing a friend can be undone.
- Streamer mode: a top-bar control to turn it on and off (also during a share), and it asks before any room or server switch while sharing.
- Menus stay on screen and highlight across their full width. A bigger shortcuts window with vim keys.

## macOS
- One title bar with the traffic lights at the left. A .dmg installer.
- Lower memory: pictures and video frames are freed (fixed a leak while sharing the screen), and memory use is about a third lower after browsing many rooms.

## Fixes
- The update check and the update button work from the tray and dialogs; in-place updates are off on NixOS.
- Rejoining a call after someone restarted their app plays audio again.
